\begin{cvparagraph}
Currently, I am a Ph.D. student in Electronic and Computer Engineering at Visual Computing Center, KAUST and supervised by Prof. Wolfgang Heidrich (IEEE Fellow). \\
\textbf{Research Focuses}: (1) Computer Graphics: 3D reconstruction via differentiable neural representation and physically-based rendering. (2) Computational Photograph: explore imaging problems by near infrared red, polarization, light field  cameras. (3) Computer Vision and Robotics: UAV-based visual tracking and segmentation. My work involves expressing, reconstructing, editing, and rendering real-world visual data to create a virtual and editable 3D world.

During my studies, I have also engaged in technical exchanges and internships at Sony, Sadeem, HKUST, and DJI. Additionally, during my doctoral studies, I have actively pursued various entrepreneurial and technology commercialization endeavors in fields such as artificial intelligence, gaming, and blockchain, as founder of SigTrading.
